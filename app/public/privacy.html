Panda — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Panda is a private, personal AI assistant operated by its owner for their own use. It is not offered to the public.

What Panda stores

  • Your account email and the chats, files, projects and settings you create in the app.
  • API keys you choose to save. They are stored so they can be used by your assistant and are never shown back in the app.
  • Browser cookies for sites you sign in to through Panda's browser, stored encrypted.

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, Panda requests only the drive.file permission. That lets it create, read and update only the files and folders Panda itself creates. Panda cannot see or change any other file in your Drive. Data obtained from Google APIs is used only to store and retrieve your own files, is not shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Panda's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Panda does not sell or share your data. Content you send to the assistant is processed by the AI model provider in order to answer you.

Deleting your data

You can delete chats, files, keys and browser logins at any time in the app. You can revoke Google Drive access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
